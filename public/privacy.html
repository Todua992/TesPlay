TeslaPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TeslaPlay is a private, self-hosted media server at teslaplay.todua.dk, run for a single household. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

What data we access

When you choose to connect a Google account, TeslaPlay requests read-only access to:

  • Google Drive (drive.readonly) to list and play video and audio files you choose to browse.
  • YouTube (youtube.readonly) to show your subscriptions, channels and playlists.

TeslaPlay never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

How data is stored

Google access tokens are stored encrypted on the TeslaPlay server and are used only to fetch content you request. File contents are streamed to your own screen and are not copied or kept. Watch history and progress inside TeslaPlay are stored on the same server.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

TeslaPlay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google at any time in TeslaPlay under Settings → Connections, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens. To delete your TeslaPlay account and its data, ask the server owner.

Contact

Questions: contact the server owner using the support email shown on Google's sign-in consent screen.
